Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email vegans_above4j@icloud.com. A screenshot, or a photo of the scale label you're scanning, helps.

Where are my sales?

Only on your iPad or iPhone. There's no account and no cloud copy, so deleting the app deletes your sales. Back up the device regularly, and export a CSV from Reports for your accountant.

How do I use my label-printing scale?

Weigh on the scale as usual and let it print the label, then scan the label in Cure's Sell screen. Set it up once:

  1. Settings → Label scale: choose whether the labels carry the price or the weight, and how many digits the item code has (usually 5).
  2. Products: give each weighed product the same PLU (item code) it has on the scale.
  3. Settings → Label scale → test a label: scan a real label and check the item code and amount are right.

Can I connect a Bluetooth scale?

Yes. Settings → Live scale → Find Bluetooth scale, then pick it. Counter scales with a serial (RS-232) port can connect through a Bluetooth-serial adapter. Without a scale link, type the weight in grams on the keypad.

What barcode scanners work?

The camera, plus any USB or Bluetooth handheld scanner that works like a keyboard. Tap the scan field on the Sell screen first, then scan.

How do I send an invoice?

After a sale, tap Share invoice (or open the sale under Sales). To bill a customer by name, add their name and email at checkout. Turn on VAT registered in Settings to print tax invoices with VAT shown.

I made a mistake on a sale

Open it under Sales and tap Void sale. The sale stays on record marked as voided, the stock goes back, and it's left out of reports.

Can I take card payments in Cure?

Take the card on your own card machine, then choose Card at checkout to record it. Built-in card payments are planned.
