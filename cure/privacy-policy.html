Cure Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Cure app for iPad and iPhone

The short version
  • Everything you put into Cure (products, sales, invoices and shop details) stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers and no cloud copy.
  • We (the developer) never receive, see, store or sell your information or your customers' information.
  • No advertising, no tracking and no analytics. Cure makes no internet connections of its own.
  • Information only leaves your device when you share it, for example sending an invoice or exporting a sales report.

1. Who we are

Cure is made by an independent developer based in South Africa ("we", "us"). For privacy questions or requests, email vegans_above4j@icloud.com.

2. What Cure stores, and where

Cure is a till for biltong shops. To do that it keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

WhatExamples
Shop detailsShop name, address, phone number, VAT number and invoice footer
ProductsNames, prices, categories, barcodes, scale item codes (PLUs) and stock levels
SalesItems sold, weights, totals, VAT, payment method, cash received and change
Customer details (optional)A customer's name and email, only if you type them in at checkout for an invoice
SettingsLabel-scale format and the Bluetooth scale you paired

We have no access to any of it.

3. Your customers' information

If you add a customer's name or email to a sale, you collect that information as the shop, and you are responsible for it under POPIA. Cure only stores it on your device and puts it on that sale's invoice. Only add customer details when the customer wants an invoice.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan product barcodes and scale labels when you choose to. Cure doesn't take or keep photos.
  • Bluetooth: to read weights from a shop scale you pair. Cure only connects to the scale you choose.

Each permission is optional. You can change it at any time in your device's Settings. Without them you can still type weights and barcodes.

5. When information leaves your device

Only when you start it:

  • Invoices. When you tap Share invoice, Cure opens your device's share sheet with a PDF. You choose who receives it and how.
  • Printing. When you tap Print, the invoice goes to the printer you pick.
  • Sales export. When you export a report, Cure creates a CSV file and you choose where to send or save it.

Once you share something, the receiving person or app handles it under their own terms. We never see it.

6. Payments

Cure doesn't process payments and never sees card details. A card payment is taken on your own card machine; Cure only records that the sale was paid by card.

7. Apple

Cure is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may collect information such as downloads, and crash and usage reports if you have allowed this in your device settings. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. When you allow sharing with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous crash and usage statistics. These never include your products, sales or customer details.

8. What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell, rent or share personal information.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking SDKs, we receive no analytics, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

9. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and encryption. Because your sales exist only on the device, keep it secure and backed up.

10. Keeping and deleting your information

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete products in the app, or delete the app, which removes everything it stored. Voided sales stay on record so your books add up. We hold no copy, so there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

11. Your rights (POPIA and similar laws)

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws, you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal information. Because Cure keeps everything on your device, you can do this directly in the app. If you have a question or concern, email us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

12. Children

Cure is a business tool and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
