Moving Up Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Applies to the Moving Up app for iPhone, iPad and Android

The short version
  • Everything you put into Moving Up stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers and no cloud copy.
  • We (the developer) never receive, see, store or sell your information.
  • No advertising and no tracking. We receive no analytics. On Android, Google's on-device text reader sends Google basic diagnostics, never your data (see section 5).
  • Your information only leaves your device when you choose to share it, for example by exporting a household snapshot to your partner.

1. Who we are

Moving Up is made by Daniel McGeorge, an individual developer based in South Africa ("we", "us"). For privacy questions or requests, email vegans_above4j@icloud.com.

2. What Moving Up stores, and where

Moving Up is a household money planner. To do that it keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

WhatExamples
Household and goalsHousehold name, member names you enter, savings goals and targets
Money recordsIncome and expense transactions, categories, notes, line items
DocumentsPhotos, PDFs and files you add (receipts, statements, letters)
Receipt and statement textText read from receipts and statements you scan, paste or pick
Bank account detailsAccount names, numbers, branch codes and payment IDs you save for transfer reminders
Plans and rewardsGame Plan steps, schedules, reward points
Settings and game progressTheme, currency, high scores

We have no access to any of it. Reading text from receipt photos and scanned PDFs happens on your device: with Apple's built-in text recognition on iPhone and iPad, and with Google's ML Kit text recognition and document scanner on Android. The images and the text read from them are not uploaded anywhere. When Moving Up can't read a receipt it may offer to email it to us so we can add that store: your own mail app opens with the receipt and the text read from it attached, and nothing is sent unless you send that email. You can turn the offer off in Settings. We use what you send only to improve the receipt reader, and delete it once that's done.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph receipts and documents when you choose to.
  • Photos: to pick existing photos or screenshots of receipts and documents.
  • Notifications: for reminders you set up, such as a transfer or debit order due date, and the play timer. They are scheduled on your device.
  • Local network: only for the two-phone games, which connect devices directly on your Wi-Fi.
  • Display over other apps (Android): to show a rainbow border around the screen when the play timer is up or a reminder goes off. It takes no touches and shows nothing else.
  • Alarms & reminders (Android): so the timer and reminders go off on time.

Each permission is optional. You can change it at any time in your device's Settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

4. When information leaves your device

Only when you start it:

  • Snapshots and sync files. When you export a household snapshot, a Game Plan update or a Connect package, Moving Up creates an encrypted file and opens your device's share sheet. You choose who receives it and how (e.g. AirDrop, Quick Share or a messaging app). Bank account details are never included in these files.
  • Sharing documents or transfer details. When you tap Share, the content goes to the app or person you pick.
  • Nearby game. Moves in a two-phone game are sent directly between the two devices on your local network.

Once you share something, the receiving person or app handles it under their own terms. We never see it.

5. Apple and Google

On iPhone and iPad, Moving Up is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may collect information such as purchases, and crash and usage reports if you have allowed this in your device settings. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. When you allow sharing with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous crash and usage statistics. These never include the contents of your Moving Up data.

On Android, Moving Up is distributed through Google Play, which handles purchases and crash and usage reports under Google's privacy policy. Moving Up reads receipts with Google's ML Kit, which runs on your device. ML Kit sends Google basic diagnostics to keep it working: the device model and Android version, the app's name and version, how long reading took, error codes, and a random per-install ID that doesn't identify you or your phone. It is sent over an encrypted connection and never includes your receipts, the text read from them, or anything you entered. Google describes this in its ML Kit data disclosure. We never receive it.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell, rent or share personal information.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking SDKs, we receive no analytics, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't link to your bank. Moving Up can't see your bank balances or move money.

7. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode, Face ID or fingerprint, and encryption. Exported snapshot files are encrypted. Because your data exists only on your device, keep your device secure and up to date.

8. Keeping and deleting your information

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete individual items in the app, reset all data in Settings, or delete the app, which removes everything it stored. We hold no copy, so there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Your rights (POPIA and similar laws)

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws, you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal information. Because Moving Up keeps everything on your device, you can do all of this directly in the app. If you have a question or concern, email us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

10. Children

Moving Up is a household planning tool and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
