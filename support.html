Moving Up Support

Questions, bugs or a receipt Moving Up can't read? Email vegans_above4j@icloud.com. For a receipt problem, a photo of the receipt helps. Cover your card number first if you'd rather not share it.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There's no account and no cloud copy, so back up or move your household with Settings → Home funds → Household → Export & Share before changing phones.

Which receipts can it read?

Open Documents and tap ⓘ to see the current list. More stores are added as sample receipts come in.

Does it move money or connect to my bank?

No. Transfer requests are reminders with the details to copy into your banking app. Tap Accept once you've paid, and Moving Up updates your goals.
