Wardrope Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email vegans_above4j@icloud.com. A screenshot of the screen helps.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There's no account and no cloud copy, so deleting the app deletes your wardrobe. Back up your iPhone or iPad to keep it when you change devices.

How do I add clothes quickly?

Tap + on the Wardrobe tab and take a photo. Or open Settings and tap ⚡ (Quick create) to paste a weekly outfit list and create the items and outfits in one go.

How do I plan an outfit for a time of day?

Planner → Calendar → Day. Drag an all-day outfit down onto the hours, then pull its corner to change how long it lasts. Tap Today to jump back.

Does Wardrope know the weather?

Not automatically. Pick how warm the day feels on Home or in the planner, and plan around it. Wardrope doesn't use your location.

How do I share my wishlist?

Home → Wishlist → the share button. It sends the list with prices and links through any messaging app.
