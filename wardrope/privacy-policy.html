Wardrope Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Applies to the Wardrope app for iPhone and iPad

The short version
  • Everything you put into Wardrope stays on your device. There are no accounts, no servers and no cloud copy.
  • We (the developer) never receive, see, store or sell your information.
  • No advertising, no tracking and no analytics. Wardrope makes no internet connections of its own.
  • Your information only leaves your device when you choose to share it, for example sending your wishlist to someone.

1. Who we are

Wardrope is made by an independent developer based in South Africa ("we", "us"). For privacy questions or requests, email vegans_above4j@icloud.com.

2. What Wardrope stores, and where

Wardrope is a wardrobe and outfit planner. To do that it keeps the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

WhatExamples
Your profileThe name you enter, and your birthday or gender if you choose to add them
Wardrobe itemsPhotos of clothes, names, categories, colours, brands, sizes and measurements, prices, wear counts
Body measurementsMeasurements you enter to prefill item sizes
Outfits and plansOutfits, the days and times you plan them, ratings, packing lists
WishlistItems you'd like, with photos, prices, shop links and notes
SettingsTheme, temperature picks, badges, custom fields

We have no access to any of it.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph clothing items, outfits and wishlist pieces when you choose to.
  • Photos: to pick existing photos for items, outfits, the wishlist or your Home background.

Each permission is optional. You can change it at any time in your device's Settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

4. When information leaves your device

Only when you start it:

  • Sharing. When you tap Share (for example on your wishlist), Wardrope opens your device's share sheet with the text and photo. You choose who receives it and how.
  • Shop links. When you tap a link you saved on a wishlist item, it opens in your browser.

Once you share something, the receiving person or app handles it under their own terms. We never see it.

5. Apple

Wardrope is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may collect information such as downloads, and crash and usage reports if you have allowed this in your device settings. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. When you allow sharing with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous crash and usage statistics. These never include the contents of your Wardrope data.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell, rent or share personal information.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking SDKs, we receive no analytics, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

7. Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and encryption. Because your data exists only on your device, keep your device secure and up to date.

8. Keeping and deleting your information

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete individual items in the app, reset your wardrobe in Settings, or delete the app, which removes everything it stored. We hold no copy, so there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Your rights (POPIA and similar laws)

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws, you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal information. Because Wardrope keeps everything on your device, you can do all of this directly in the app. If you have a question or concern, email us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

10. Children

Wardrope isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
